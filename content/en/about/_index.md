## About Us

Yopitek Ltd (榆閤科技有限公司) is a technology company headquartered in Taipei, Taiwan, specializing in the planning, design, and integration of professional projection systems. We are dedicated to merging artistic creativity with advanced projection technology to deliver stunning visual experiences for government agencies, museums, corporations, and cultural events.

From architectural projection mapping on landmark buildings to permanent interactive museum installations, from large-scale immersive event environments to corporate brand exhibitions — Yopitek brings extensive hands-on expertise and precise technical execution to every project, making us a trusted projection integration partner in Taiwan. Our services cover the entire island, with completed projects across Taipei, Taoyuan, Tainan, Yilan, and Pingtung.

## Two Business Divisions

### IT_Application — Immersive Experience Integration

The IT_Application division delivers a full turnkey service: requirements consulting, site assessment, equipment selection, system installation, content planning, on-site commissioning, and long-term maintenance.

### IT_Distribution — Technology Product Distribution

Authorized distributor for world-leading technology brands, serving Taiwan enterprises, security research organizations, and academic institutions with genuine products and expert technical support.

## Our Strengths

**Full-Scale Projection Capability**
From compact museum galleries measuring tens of square meters to entire large-scale building facades — we plan and execute projection systems at every venue scale.

**Multi-Projector Edge-Blending Expertise**
Precision calibration across multiple projectors for seamless edge blending on flat screens, ultra-wide curved surfaces, and corner-wrap configurations.

**Trusted by Government and Classified Environments**
We have successfully deployed systems for defense agencies (Military Police Command) and multiple government bodies, with proven experience in strict confidentiality requirements and non-standard deployment environments.

**Comprehensive Turnkey Service**
We cover the entire project lifecycle: needs analysis, site measurement, equipment specification, system installation, content planning, commissioning, and ongoing maintenance.
