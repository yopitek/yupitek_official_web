Advanced Card Systems (ACS) is a globally recognized manufacturer of smart card readers. The ACR series is widely deployed in access control, digital signatures, NFC mobile payments, and identity verification systems.

The ACS ACR series is a family of USB NFC smart card readers made by Advanced Card Systems (ACS). It covers the ACR122U, the NFC Forum certified ACR1252U and the fourth-generation ACR1552U, all for 13.56 MHz cards. Yopitek offers the series in Taiwan; contact us for a quotation.
