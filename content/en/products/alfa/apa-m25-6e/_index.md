## Product Overview

The APA-M25-6E is a WiFi 6E tri-band indoor directional antenna from ALFA Network, with full support for 2.4 GHz / 5 GHz / 6 GHz (including the WiFi 6E dedicated band 5.975 ~ 7.125 GHz). It shares the same form factor as the APA-M25 (167.3 × 66 × 18 mm) for a seamless drop-in upgrade. When paired with the AWUS036AXM or AWUS036AXML, the 6 GHz band can deliver up to **1200 Mbps** connection speeds.

> **Spec Note**: The vertical beamwidth is listed as 45° on the official website and 40° in the PDF datasheet — refer to the product batch label for the definitive value.

## Key Features

- **Full WiFi 6E Tri-Band Coverage**: A single antenna covers 2.4 / 5 / 6 GHz
- **9 dBi Gain at 6 GHz**: Compensates for higher indoor attenuation of the 6 GHz short wavelength
- **Directional Panel Design**: 60° horizontal beamwidth, effectively reduces multipath interference
- **Direct APA-M25 Replacement**: Same physical dimensions, seamless upgrade path
- **RP-SMA Plug Standard Connector**: Compatible with all ALFA WiFi 6E adapters
