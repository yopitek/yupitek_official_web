## Product Overview

The ARS-25-57A is a dual-band paddle (paddle-style) omni-directional antenna from ALFA Network, supporting both 2.4 GHz (5 dBi) and 5 GHz (**7 dBi**) simultaneously. Its flat wide-blade form factor uses an omni-directional radiation pattern for 360° uniform horizontal coverage — no aiming required, perfect for placement at the center of a desk. The RP-SMA Male connector directly replaces the factory antennas on all ALFA dual-band adapters.

## Key Features

- **Paddle Flat Design**: Larger cross-section than traditional round dipole antennas for better low-frequency radiation efficiency
- **5 GHz 7 dBi Gain**: Effectively compensates for 5 GHz range attenuation loss
- **Omni-Directional Radiation**: 360° uniform horizontal coverage, no aiming needed
- **Direct Plug-In Replacement**: RP-SMA Male connector, directly replaces factory antenna
- **Full 5 GHz Channel Coverage**: Covers 5.1 ~ 5.9 GHz (including DFS channels)
- **Use Cases**: Desktop office, remote work — stable dual-band connectivity for everyday use
