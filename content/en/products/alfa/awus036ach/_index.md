## Product Overview

The AWUS036ACH is one of ALFA Network's most popular AC1200 dual-band USB adapters in the security research community. Powered by the Realtek RTL8812AU chipset, it supports IEEE 802.11ac with 2.4GHz 300 Mbps + 5GHz 867 Mbps throughput. Features dual replaceable 5 dBi RP-SMA antennas, USB 3.0 interface, and full Monitor Mode and Packet Injection support — a top choice for Kali Linux users.
