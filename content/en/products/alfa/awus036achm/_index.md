## Product Overview

The AWUS036ACHM is powered by the MediaTek MT7610U chipset and supports IEEE 802.11ac dual-band with a maximum throughput of 433 Mbps on 5GHz. Its compact single-antenna design with a 1m USB extension cable makes it ideal for portable or low-power use cases.
