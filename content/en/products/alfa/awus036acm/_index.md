## Product Overview

The AWUS036ACM is ALFA Network's classic flagship adapter, powered by the MediaTek MT7612U chipset. It supports IEEE 802.11ac dual-band operation, delivering 300 Mbps on 2.4GHz and 867 Mbps on 5GHz. Equipped with two 5 dBi RP-SMA detachable antennas and a USB 3.0 interface, it is a top choice for security researchers.
