## Product Overview

The AWUS036AXER is the lightest Wi-Fi 6 adapter in the ALFA lineup, powered by the Realtek RTL8832BU chipset. Its body measures just 65 × 24 × 10 mm and weighs only 10.5 g, featuring an integrated 2×2 dual-band antenna design (no external antenna). Its ultra-compact form factor is ideal for everyday carry or scenarios where an external antenna is not required.
