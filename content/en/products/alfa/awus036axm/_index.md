## Product Overview

The AWUS036AXM features the MediaTek MT7921AUN chipset with Wi-Fi 6E tri-band support (2.4 GHz / 5 GHz / 6 GHz), reaching up to 3000 Mbps combined, plus Bluetooth 5.2 with an integrated BT antenna. The L-elbow USB-A connector avoids blocking adjacent ports. Includes 2× 5 dBi detachable RP-SMA antennas.

> **macOS Notice:** All ALFA adapters have limited/no macOS support. macOS 11 Big Sur and later, and Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3) are **NOT** supported. Maximum macOS support is 10.15 Catalina on Intel Macs.

## Key Features

- Wi-Fi 6E Tri-Band: 2.4 / 5 / 6 GHz
- MediaTek MT7921AUN chipset
- Up to 3000 Mbps combined throughput
- Bluetooth 5.2 (integrated BT antenna + LED indicator)
- USB-A L-elbow connector (USB 3.2 Gen 1, 5 Gbps)
- 2× RP-SMA female detachable antennas (5 dBi)
- WPA3/WPA2/WPA/WEP/WPS
- Kali Linux Monitor Mode + Packet Injection
