## Product Overview

The AWUS036AXM is ALFA Network's Wi-Fi 6E flagship wireless adapter, powered by the MediaTek MT7921AUN chipset. It supports tri-band operation across 2.4GHz, 5GHz, and 6GHz (6GHz band availability subject to regional regulations) and integrates Bluetooth 5.2. It features two RP-SMA external antennas and a USB 3.2 Gen 1 interface.
