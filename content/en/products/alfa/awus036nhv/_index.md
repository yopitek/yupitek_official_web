## Product Overview

The AWUS036NHV is ALFA's high-power single-band 2.4GHz wireless adapter, supporting IEEE 802.11 b/g/n with a maximum throughput of 150 Mbps. Its high-power design (exact output subject to regional regulations) and 5 dBi RP-SMA external antenna make it well-suited for long-range 2.4GHz wireless connectivity and Kali Linux security research environments.
