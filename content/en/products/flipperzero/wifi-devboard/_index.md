The WiFi Devboard is the official Wi-Fi expansion board for Flipper Zero, built on the ESP32-S2 chipset. It is intended for authorized security research and education by users who already own a Flipper Zero. Yopitek offers Flipper Zero products in Taiwan.

## Product Overview

The WiFi Devboard is the official Wi-Fi expansion module for Flipper Zero, built on the ESP32-S2 Chipset and connected via GPIO. It adds 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi capability to the host device. When paired with the community Marauder Firmware, it enables Wi-Fi security testing functions in authorized testing environments only.

| Item | Specification |
