## Product Overview

The ASUS PRIME RTX5080-16G features the NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5080 GPU with 16GB GDDR7 VRAM and a PCIe 5.0 interface, delivering next-generation AI acceleration, ray tracing, and DLSS 4 super-resolution technology. The PRIME series offers a clean, reliable design suited for workstations and general professional applications.

## Key Specifications

| Item | Specification |
|------|---------------|
| GPU | NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5080 |
| VRAM | 16GB GDDR7 |
| Interface | PCIe 5.0 x16 |
| Outputs | 3× DisplayPort 2.1 + 1× HDMI 2.1 |
| Power Connector | 16-pin (PCIe 5.0) |
