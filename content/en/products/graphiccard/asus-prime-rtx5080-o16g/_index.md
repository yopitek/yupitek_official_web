## Product Overview

The ASUS PRIME RTX5080-O16G is the overclocked (OC) version of the PRIME RTX5080-16G, featuring factory-boosted core and memory clocks to deliver higher performance than reference specifications. It is suited for applications that demand the maximum performance from the RTX 5080.
