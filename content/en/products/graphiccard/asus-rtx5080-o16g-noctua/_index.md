## Product Overview

The ASUS RTX5080 O16G NOCTUA is a collaboration product between ASUS and Austria's premier silent cooling brand Noctua. It uses Noctua's industrial-grade brown silent fans to deliver near-silent thermal performance at flagship GPU speeds. Ideal for noise-sensitive professional environments such as museums, broadcast studios, and silent workstations.
