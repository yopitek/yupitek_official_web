## Product Overview

The MSI RTX5080 16G SUPRIM LIQUID SOC is MSI's all-in-one (AIO) liquid-cooled flagship graphics card, integrating the liquid cooling system directly into the card body for near-silent thermal performance. It is the ideal solution for noise-sensitive workstations, broadcast studios, and museum installations.

## Key Specifications

| Item | Specification |
|------|--------------|
| GPU | NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5080 (Overclocked) |
| VRAM | 16 GB GDDR7 |
| Cooling | All-in-one liquid cooling (LIQUID) |
| Series | MSI SUPRIM Flagship |
| Interface | PCIe 5.0 x16 |

## Liquid Cooling Advantages

- Quieter than air cooling — suitable for recording studios and broadcast environments
- More stable thermal performance for sustained rendering workloads
- Reduces airflow demand inside the chassis
