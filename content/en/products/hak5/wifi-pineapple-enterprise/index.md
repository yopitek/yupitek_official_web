## Product Features

![WiFi Pineapple Enterprise](/images/products/hak5/card/wifi-pineapple-enterprise.png)

- **Flagship Enterprise WiFi Audit Platform**: Designed for enterprises, security institutions, and long-term deployments
- **Powerful Hardware**: Quad-core ARM CPU, five dual-band MIMO radios covering 2.4 GHz / 5 GHz
- **High-Density Client Support**: Up to 100 DHCP clients (vs. 5–10 for the Mark VII)
- **Dual GigE Interfaces**: Dual Gigabit Ethernet physical network interfaces for high-throughput backhaul
- **Industrial-Grade Chassis**: Metal enclosure, 1U rack-mountable, supports AC 100–240V power (50/60 Hz)
- **Advanced Wireless Technology**: Supports MU-MIMO, TxBF, Qualcomm Wi-Fi SON
- **PineAP Suite**: Supports automated Campaigns and Cloud C²
- **PtaaS Service**: Cobalt PtaaS (Pentest as a Service) joint service option available
