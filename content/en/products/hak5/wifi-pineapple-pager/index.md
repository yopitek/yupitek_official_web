## Product Overview

The WiFi Pineapple Pager is HAK5's 8th-generation PineAP flagship in a pocket-sized form factor, combining two decades of Wi-Fi exploitation expertise with modern payload technology. Unlike traditional tethered tools, the Pager operates completely standalone with a built-in 2000 mAh battery, a 2.4-inch color display, and real-time vibration, ringtone, and on-screen alerts for wireless activity. It is the first WiFi Pineapple to support DuckyScript and Bash payloads, with full tri-band coverage across 2.4 GHz, 5 GHz, and 6 GHz.
