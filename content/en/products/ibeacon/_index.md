Yupitek iBeacon products represent a new generation of Bluetooth® Low Energy (BLE 5.0) beacon devices designed for high-performance localization, personnel tracking, and commercial advertising. Supporting both Apple iBeacon™ and Google Eddystone™ (UID, URL, TLM) protocols, our beacons allow simultaneous multi-protocol broadcasts (up to 6 slots) configured easily via the BeaconSET+ mobile app.

---

## BLE Beacon System Topology

![BLE Beacon System Topology Diagram](/images/products/ibeacon/ibeacon_topology.png)

Our iBeacon ecosystem connects physical locations with enterprise cloud networks. The beacons transmit BLE radio signals at regular intervals, which are scanned by mobile devices (running corporate apps) and BLE Gateways, forwarding secure logs to central attendance and asset tracking systems.
