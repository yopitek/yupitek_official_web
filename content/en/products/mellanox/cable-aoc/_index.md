# NVIDIA Mellanox LinkX Active Optical Cables (AOC) — 100G & 200G

NVIDIA LinkX® Active Optical Cables (AOC) combine transceiver components and fiber cabling into a single, factory-terminated assembly. AOCs are designed for longer distances than copper DACs (up to 80 meters) while being lighter, thinner, and more flexible, making them ideal for high-density cable management within and across server rows.

## Technical Comparison: DAC vs AOC

Choosing between Direct Attach Copper (DAC) and Active Optical Cables (AOC) depends on physical layout and airflow considerations:

### 1. Distance & Reach
- **DAC**: Capped at 5m due to electrical attenuation over copper.
- **AOC**: Supports lengths up to 80–100m, allowing connection of servers in different racks or rows.

### 2. Cable Weight & Thickness
- **DAC**: Copper wire bundle is thick and heavy. 5m DAC bundles can weigh several kilograms, putting stress on cable management arms and blocking chassis exhaust airflow.
- **AOC**: Extremely thin, flexible glass fiber. Minimal weight and airflow obstruction.

### 3. Latency & Power
- **DAC (Passive)**: Near-zero latency and 0W power consumption.
- **AOC**: Optical conversion engines at each connector shell introduce a tiny fraction of latency (negligible in most workloads) and consume approximately 1–1.5W per plug end.
