Using original LinkX fiber cables ensures that connectors, ferrule polishes, and glass quality meet the strict insertion loss and return loss tolerances required for high-speed Ethernet and InfiniBand networking.

## Technical Guide: Fiber Cable Specifications

### 1. OM4 Multimode Fiber (MMF)
- **Core Diameter**: 50µm (micrometer) core with 125µm cladding.
- **Color Standard**: Aqua/Erika Violet.
- **Bandwidth**: Optimized for VCSEL lasers operating at 850nm. OM4 supports distances up to 100m at 100Gb/s (SR4) or 150m at 25Gb/s (SR).

### 2. MPO-12 Connectors
- **Pin Configuration**: MPO connectors are gendered. Transceivers have pins (male), so patch cables must be **pinless / female** (incorporating guide holes) to mate successfully.
- **End-face Polish**: Standard MPO connectors use **PC / UPC** (flat) polish. High-speed singlemode MPO and modern 400G/800G multimode OSFP/QSFP112 transceivers require **APC (Angled Physical Contact, 8-degree slant)** polish to eliminate back reflection. APC connectors are identified by green connector bodies.

### 3. MPO Polarity Standards
For MPO-12 networks, polarity ensures that the transmitter on one end connects to the receiver on the other:
- **Type-A (Key Up to Key Down)**: Straight-through wiring. Pin 1 connects to Pin 1.
- **Type-B (Key Up to Key Up)**: Reversed wiring. Pin 1 connects to Pin 12. **This is the industry standard for connecting two SR4 transceivers directly.**
- **Type-C (Pair-wise Flipped)**: Pin 1/2, 3/4 are flipped. Used primarily in structured backbone trunking.
