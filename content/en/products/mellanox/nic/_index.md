## Technical Selection Guide

When selecting your ConnectX adapter card, pay close attention to:

### 1. Protocol Mode (VPI vs EN)
- **EN Adapters** only support Ethernet networks.
- **VPI (Virtual Protocol Interconnect) Adapters** can be configured via firmware to operate either as an InfiniBand or Ethernet card, providing maximum deployment flexibility.

### 2. PCIe Bandwidth Requirements
Ensure your host server's PCIe version and slot width can support the card at full speeds:
- A dual-port 100G NIC requires PCIe 4.0 x16 to run both ports concurrently at full capacity. 
- Connecting a PCIe 4.0 card into a PCIe 3.0 slot is backward compatible, but the throughput will be capped at the PCIe 3.0 limit (approx. 64Gbps for x8, 128Gbps for x16).

### 3. OCP 3.0 vs Standard PCIe Form Factor
Models with suffixes like `-ADAB`, `-CDAB`, `-HEAB` use the **OCP NIC 3.0** form factor. These cards slide into dedicated server bays (common in Supermicro, Dell, HPE, and Lenovo newer generations) and cannot be fitted into a standard PCIe slot.
