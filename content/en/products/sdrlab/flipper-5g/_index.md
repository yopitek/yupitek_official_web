## Features

![SDRLab Flipper Zero 5G Add-On Board](/images/products/sdrlab/flipper-5g.png)

- Based on the AI Thinker BW16 module (Realtek RTL8720DN chip) with native 5 GHz Wi-Fi support
- Dual-band coverage (2.4 GHz + 5 GHz) for probing modern dual-band wireless environments
- Pre-flashed 5G Wi-Fi Deauthentication firmware — plug in and go
- Powered directly from Flipper Zero GPIO — no external power supply needed
- Supports mesh network topology identification and wireless environment scanning
- Compatible with the Momentum and Unleashed firmware frameworks
- PlatformIO support for custom firmware development and flashing
- Cortex-M0 low-power core extends field operation time
