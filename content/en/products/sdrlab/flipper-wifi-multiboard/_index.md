## Product Overview

The SDRLab Flipper Zero WiFi Multi-Board integrates an ESP8266 Wi-Fi module and connects via Flipper Zero's GPIO interface to provide Wi-Fi connectivity. This enables Flipper Zero to execute network-dependent functions such as remote control, data upload, and Wi-Fi-based research tasks.
