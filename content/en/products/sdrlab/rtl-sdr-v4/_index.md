## Product Overview

The RTL-SDR Blog RTL SDR V4 is one of the most popular entry-level Software Defined Radio receivers on the market. This fourth-generation upgrade improves low-frequency reception performance and image rejection compared to the V3. It supports wideband reception from 500kHz to 1766MHz and, when paired with free software such as SDR#, GQRX, or GNU Radio, can receive FM broadcasts, ADS-B aviation signals, AIS maritime traffic, weather satellite imagery, and a wide variety of other RF signals.

## Key Specifications

| Parameter | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| Frequency Range | 500kHz – 1766MHz |
| ADC Resolution | 8-bit |
| Sample Rate | Up to 3.2 MSPS |
| Interface | USB 2.0 |
| Antenna Connector | SMA female |
| Driver | librtlsdr / rtl-sdr |

## Applications

- FM broadcast reception
- ADS-B aircraft tracking
- AIS vessel tracking
- Weather satellite imagery (NOAA, Meteor-M)
- Amateur radio monitoring
- RF signal analysis
