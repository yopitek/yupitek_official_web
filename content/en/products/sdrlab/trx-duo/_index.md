## Product Overview

The SDRLab TRX-duo is a high-performance dual-channel SDR platform based on the Xilinx ZYNQ7010. It delivers 16-bit ADC sampling precision and is compatible with Red Pitaya ecosystem software, supporting full transceiver (TRX) operation. It is well-suited for university research labs, engineers, and advanced amateur radio operators conducting RF communications research and experimentation.

## Key Specifications

| Parameter | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| Processor | Xilinx ZYNQ7010 |
| ADC | Dual-channel 16-bit |
| Function | Transceiver (TRX) |
| Compatibility | Red Pitaya ecosystem |
