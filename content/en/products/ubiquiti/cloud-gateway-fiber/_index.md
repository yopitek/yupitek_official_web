## Product Features

![Ubiquiti UniFi Cloud Gateway Fiber](/images/products/ubiquiti/cloud-gateway-fiber.png)

- Compact gateway optimized for fiber ISP connectivity, with dual SFP+ + 10 GbE RJ45 WAN ports
- IDS/IPS throughput 5 Gbps with 55,000+ CyberSecure signatures
- Optional NVMe SSD storage (up to 2 TB) with integrated UniFi Protect NVR
- Manages 50+ UniFi devices, 500+ simultaneous users, and 50 access hubs
- Supports up to 6 WAN ports with multi-WAN load balancing and failover
- Powered by a Cortex-A73 quad-core processor for higher performance than previous generations
- Features a 0.96" LCM status display and Bluetooth setup
