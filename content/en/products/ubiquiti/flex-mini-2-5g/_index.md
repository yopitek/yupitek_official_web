## Product Features

![Ubiquiti UniFi Flex Mini 2.5G](/images/products/ubiquiti/flex-mini-2-5g.png)

- 5-port 2.5 GbE Layer 2 managed switch with 25 Gbps switching capacity
- Powers via USB-C (5V) or PoE input — no separate power cable required
- Ultra-slim desktop design (206 g), ideal for space-constrained environments
- Supports 256 VLANs and a 4,000-entry MAC address table
- Full L2 feature set: STP/RSTP, port mirroring, storm control, flow control
- Wide operating temperature range: -20 to 45°C, suitable for industrial environments
- NDAA compliant, CE/FCC/IC/Anatel certified
