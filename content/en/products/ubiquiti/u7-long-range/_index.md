## Product Features

![Ubiquiti UniFi U7 Long-Range](/images/products/ubiquiti/u7-long-range.png)

- Wi-Fi 7 dual-band (2.4 / 5 GHz) long-range access point with 5 spatial streams (3+2)
- 5 GHz 3×3 MU-MIMO with maximum TX power of 27 dBm for outstanding long-range coverage
- Coverage area 160 m², powered by standard PoE (no PoE+ required)
- Lightweight design: 175.7 mm diameter / 448 g
- Wide operating temperature range: -30 to 50°C
- Includes plastic mounting bracket for ceiling or wall installation
- NDAA compliant, CE/FCC/IC/SRRC/Anatel certified
