## Product Overview

The UniFi U7 Long Range features a high-gain directional antenna design that provides significantly extended signal range compared to standard APs. It is ideal for warehouses, factories, parking structures, and other large open-space environments, enabling broad wireless coverage with fewer access points.

## Key Specifications

| Item | Specification |
|------|--------------|
| Wi-Fi Standard | IEEE 802.11be (Wi-Fi 7) |
| Band | Dual-band: 2.4 GHz + 5 GHz |
| Wired Interface | 2.5G RJ45 (PoE+ powered) |
| Power | PoE+ (802.3at) |
