## Product Overview

The UniFi U7 Pro Max is the high-power advanced version of the U7 Pro, designed with a higher output power to provide a wider wireless coverage area. It is well suited for lobbies, exhibition halls, large offices, and other spaces requiring an extended coverage radius.

## Key Specifications

| Item | Specification |
|------|---------------|
| Wi-Fi Standard | IEEE 802.11be (Wi-Fi 7) |
| Bands | Tri-Band: 2.4GHz + 5GHz + 6GHz |
| Wired Interface | 2.5G RJ45 (PoE++ powered) |
| Power Input | PoE++ (802.3bt) |
