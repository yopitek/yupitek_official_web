## Product Features

![Ubiquiti UniFi U7 Pro Max](/images/products/ubiquiti/u7-pro-max.png)

- Wi-Fi 7 tri-band access point with 8 spatial streams (2+4+2); 5 GHz upgraded to 4×4 MU-MIMO
- 5 GHz maximum data rate 8.6 Gbps, coverage area 160 m²
- Up to 500+ simultaneous client connections, optimized for high-density environments
- Built-in Real-Time Spectral Analysis to actively detect wireless interference
- Aluminum alloy enclosure for superior thermal performance
- 2.5 GbE uplink, PoE+ powered
- NDAA compliant, CE/FCC/IC certified

## Applications

Designed for high-density large venues such as convention centers, exhibition halls, and large enterprise floors. The 5 GHz 4×4 MU-MIMO configuration is suited for scenarios where each AP is expected to serve 500+ clients with heavy 5 GHz band congestion. Real-Time Spectral Analysis actively detects and avoids wireless interference.
