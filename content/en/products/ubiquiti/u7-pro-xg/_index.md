## Product Overview

The UniFi U7 Pro XG builds on the U7 Pro specifications by upgrading to a 10G RJ45 wired uplink interface. It is designed for enterprise environments already equipped with a 10G backbone network, ensuring the wired uplink of the wireless AP never becomes a bottleneck.

## Key Specifications

| Item | Specification |
|------|---------------|
| Wi-Fi Standard | IEEE 802.11be (Wi-Fi 7) |
| Bands | Tri-Band: 2.4GHz + 5GHz + 6GHz |
| Wired Interface | 10G RJ45 |
| Power Input | PoE++ (802.3bt) |
