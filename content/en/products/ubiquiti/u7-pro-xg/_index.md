## Product Features

![Ubiquiti UniFi U7 Pro XG](/images/products/ubiquiti/u7-pro-xg.png)

- Wi-Fi 7 tri-band access point with a 10 GbE uplink port, breaking the 2.5 GbE bottleneck
- Ultra-slim design at only 32.5 mm thick, includes Lite Mount
- 6 spatial streams (2+2+2), coverage area 140 m²
- Available in white and black
- PoE+ powered (42.5–57V DC), maximum power consumption 22W
- Supports 8 BSSIDs per radio with full enterprise security features
- NDAA compliant, CE/FCC/IC certified

## Applications

Designed for enterprise and commercial environments that have deployed or are building a 10 GbE wired infrastructure. When multiple APs are densely installed under an aggregation switch, the 10 GbE uplink effectively removes the 2.5 GbE bandwidth bottleneck and enables higher aggregate throughput.
