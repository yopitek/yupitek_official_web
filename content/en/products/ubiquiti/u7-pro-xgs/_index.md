## Product Features

![Ubiquiti UniFi U7 Pro XGS](/images/products/ubiquiti/u7-pro-xgs.png)

- UniFi flagship AP: 10 GbE uplink + PoE++ power + 8 spatial streams (2+4+2)
- The ultimate combination of 4×4 5 GHz MU-MIMO (8.6 Gbps) and 10 GbE uplink
- Supports Zero-Wait DFS, eliminating channel-switching delays on DFS channels
- Built-in Real-Time Spectral Analysis
- UV-stabilized polycarbonate + aluminum alloy enclosure for demanding deployments
- Up to 500+ simultaneous client connections, coverage area 160 m²
- NDAA compliant, CE/FCC/IC certified
