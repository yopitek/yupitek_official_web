## Product Features

![Ubiquiti UniFi U7 Pro](/images/products/ubiquiti/u7-pro.png)

- Wi-Fi 7 tri-band access point supporting 2.4 / 5 / 6 GHz with 6 spatial streams
- Coverage area 140 m², up to 300+ simultaneous client connections
- 2.5 GbE uplink, PoE+ powered
- Supports 802.11r fast roaming, PPSK, RADIUS/RadSec, and Passpoint
- Supports 8 BSSIDs per radio, including guest isolation and network scheduling
- Includes Pro Mount for ceiling or wall installation
- NDAA compliant, CE/FCC/IC certified
