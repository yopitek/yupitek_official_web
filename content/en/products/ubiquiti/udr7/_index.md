## Product Overview

The UniFi Dream Router 7 (UDR7) is the Wi-Fi 7 upgrade of the Dream Router series, consolidating router, switch, UniFi controller, and AP into a single unit. It is ideal for home or small office environments looking to build a complete UniFi ecosystem without additional controller hardware.

## Key Specifications

| Item | Specification |
|------|--------------|
| Wi-Fi Standard | IEEE 802.11be (Wi-Fi 7) |
| Band | Tri-band: 2.4 GHz + 5 GHz + 6 GHz |
| Wired Ports | WAN + LAN (with PoE output) |
| Built-in Controller | UniFi Network Controller |
