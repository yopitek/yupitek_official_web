## Product Overview

The UniFi Switch Lite 16 PoE is a 16-port gigabit managed PoE switch designed for small-to-medium network environments. It provides 8 PoE/PoE+ ports for powering IP phones, wireless access points, and IP cameras, plus 8 standard gigabit ports and 2 SFP fiber uplinks — all managed centrally through the UniFi Network App.

Its desktop/rack-mount convertible design and fanless operation make it suitable for both office desks and 1U rack installations.

## Key Features

- 8 × PoE/PoE+ gigabit ports (IEEE 802.3af/at) + 8 × standard gigabit ports
- 2 × SFP 1G fiber uplinks for high-bandwidth or long-distance connections
- Total PoE budget: 45W — powers multiple APs, cameras, or VoIP phones simultaneously
- 32 Gbps switching capacity — non-blocking performance for full port throughput
- Fanless silent design — ideal for quiet office, classroom, or desk deployments
- Full Layer 2 feature set: VLAN, QoS, port mirroring, storm control, STP/RSTP
- Seamless integration with the UniFi ecosystem — managed alongside APs and gateways in one app
- Compact desktop and 1U rack-mountable (rack ears optional)

## Applications

The Switch Lite 16 PoE is the right choice when you need to expand PoE device density in a UniFi network without rack infrastructure. Common use cases:

- **Small offices** needing PoE ports for Wi-Fi 6/7 APs, VoIP phones, and IP cameras from a single managed switch
- **Classrooms / meeting rooms** requiring clean, fanless operation with VLAN segmentation for staff vs. guest networks
- **Retail environments** where a compact footprint and UniFi app management save IT overhead
- **Network closet upgrades** from unmanaged to fully managed switching with zero-touch provisioning
