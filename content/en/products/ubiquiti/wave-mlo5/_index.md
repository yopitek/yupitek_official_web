## Product Features

![Ubiquiti Wave MLO5](/images/products/ubiquiti/wave-mlo5.png)

- Wi-Fi 7 dual 5 GHz wireless backhaul (Multi-Link Operation), compatible with 5 GHz bands worldwide
- Total throughput 5+ Gbps, maximum transmission distance 100 km
- Built-in GPS antenna for precise alignment and timing
- IPX6 weatherproofing for all-weather outdoor deployment
- Equipped with (1) 10 GbE RJ45 + (1) 10G SFP+ dual uplink ports
- WPA3-PSK (AES) encryption, NDAA compliant
- CE certified, suitable for global markets

Designed for ISP and enterprise point-to-point wireless backhaul in regions without 6 GHz spectrum licensing. Operating on dual 5 GHz radios, it delivers 5+ Gbps throughput at distances up to 100 km. Suitable for outdoor pole-mounted installations; CE certification ensures compliance for European and global markets. Centrally managed via the UISP platform.
