## Product Features

![Ubiquiti Wave MLO6](/images/products/ubiquiti/wave-mlo6.png)

- Wi-Fi 7 dual-band wireless backhaul, supporting 5 GHz + 6 GHz simultaneously (Multi-Link Operation)
- Total throughput 8+ Gbps, maximum transmission distance 100 km
- Built-in GPS antenna for precise alignment and timing
- IPX6 weatherproofing for all-weather outdoor deployment
- Equipped with (1) 10 GbE RJ45 + (1) 10G SFP+ dual uplink ports
- WPA3-PSK (AES) encryption, NDAA compliant
- Managed by UISP platform
