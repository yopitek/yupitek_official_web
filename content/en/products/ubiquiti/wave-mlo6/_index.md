## Product Overview

The Wave MLO6 is the 6 GHz band variant of the Wave MLO5. By leveraging the lower interference characteristics of the 6 GHz spectrum, it provides more stable high-speed point-to-point wireless backbone connectivity in electromagnetically complex urban environments.

## Key Specifications

| Item | Specification |
|------|--------------|
| Band | 6 GHz millimeter wave |
| Technology | Multi-Link Operation (MLO) |
| Wired Interface | 10G SFP+ |
| Application | Point-to-point bridge |
