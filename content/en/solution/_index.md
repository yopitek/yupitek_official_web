## About Yopitek IT_Application Division

Yopitek Ltd (榆閤科技有限公司) is a Taipei, Taiwan-based technology company specializing in the planning, design, and integration of professional projection systems. We leverage advanced projection technology to seamlessly unite artistic vision with engineering precision, delivering breathtaking visual environments for government agencies, museums, corporations, and cultural events.

**02 · Rong Cheng Paper Industry Eco Exhibition**
*Rong Cheng Paper Industry Eco Exhibition — World Trade Center*

![世貿榮成紙業環保展](/images/solution/case-rong-cheng.webp)

{{< badge >}}Corporate Immersive Exhibition{{< /badge >}} {{< badge >}}Client: Rong Cheng Paper{{< /badge >}}

This project created a multi-wall surround immersive projection exhibition room inside the Taipei World Trade Center, custom-designed for Rong Cheng Paper's annual eco-image exhibition. **03 · Color Taipei — City Hall Projection Mapping**
*2021 Color Taipei — Taipei City Hall Projection Mapping*

![彩虹台北市政府光雕](/images/solution/case-color-taipei.webp)

{{< badge >}}Architectural Projection Mapping{{< /badge >}} {{< badge >}}Client: Taipei City Government{{< /badge >}}

<details><summary>View Case Details</summary>

This project used the entire facade of Taipei City Hall as the projection canvas, presenting large-scale dynamic artistic projection that fused Taiwan's multicultural diversity with rainbow pride imagery to coincide with the 2021 "Color Taipei" rainbow festival. Yopitek overcame the complex geometric correction challenges of a large building's intricate facade, precisely aligning every architectural surface including colonnades, porticos, and multi-layered recessed window structures. The show ran for several consecutive nights, each performance flawlessly executed. It drew tens of thousands of Taipei residents and was widely covered by major media, becoming one of the city's landmark public art projects of the year.

**04 · Houbi Orchid Exhibition**
*Houbi Orchid Exhibition — Large Curved Panoramic Projection*

![後壁蘭花展](/images/solution/case-houbi-orchid.webp)

{{< badge >}}Large Curved Panoramic Projection{{< /badge >}} {{< badge >}}Client: Tainan City Government{{< /badge >}}

The Houbi Orchid Exhibition is one of Taiwan's most important international floral events. Yopitek was commissioned to produce the ultra-large curved projection for the core immersive scene in the main exhibition hall. The total projection screen length exceeded 64 meters, designed in an arc-shaped wraparound configuration using multiple high-brightness projectors with precise seamless edge-blending calibration. The result was a fluid, fully immersive dynamic display of tropical plant communities, jungle ecosystem rhythms, and blooming florals in all their vivid glory. Paired with the artist's suspended sculptural installations and carefully designed ambient lighting, the entire exhibition hall was transformed into a living tropical botanical garden, drawing every visitor naturally into the gentle embrace of nature.

**06 · Banqiao Radio Station**
*Banqiao Radio Station — Full 360° Immersive Projection Room*

![板橋放送所 360° 沉浸式投影空間](/images/solution/case-banqiao-radio.webp)

{{< badge >}}360° Full-Immersive Projection Space{{< /badge >}} {{< badge >}}Client: Banqiao Cultural Center{{< /badge >}}

**07 · Yilan Water Museum**
*Yilan Water Museum — Corner Wall Projection Display*

![宜蘭自來水博物館 轉角投影](/images/solution/case-yilan-water.webp)

{{< badge >}}Museum Corner Seamless Projection{{< /badge >}} {{< badge >}}Client: Yilan County Government{{< /badge >}}

The Yilan Water Museum exhibition room employs Yopitek's specialized corner seamless projection technology to present cohesive water resource science education animated graphics across interior corner walls. **11 · Pingtung Donggang King's Boat Museum**
*Pingtung Donggang King's Boat Museum — Panoramic Immersive Projection Room*

![屏東東港王船博物館 環形沉浸式投影空間](/images/solution/case-king-boat.jpg)

{{< badge >}}Cultural Museum Circular Immersive Projection{{< /badge >}} {{< badge >}}Client: Donggang King's Boat Museum{{< /badge >}}

The Pingtung Donggang King Boat Festival is one of Taiwan's most representative intangible cultural heritage events, listed as an important national folk custom. To convey this precious traditional belief culture to contemporary audiences in a more impactful modern way, Yopitek was commissioned to create a large circular immersive projection space in the museum's core exhibition hall, presenting the deities, ocean imagery, and folk art of the King Boat Festival in full panoramic display. The project deployed multiple high-brightness professional projectors in a wraparound configuration, combined with precision edge-blending technology, to seamlessly project richly colored, detail-filled traditional folk art animated imagery onto the entire curved arc exhibition wall. Visitors standing within the space experience an intense visual impact and deep cultural resonance.
