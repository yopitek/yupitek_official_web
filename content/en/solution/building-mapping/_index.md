## Building Projection Mapping

Building Mapping transforms architectural facades into dynamic visual canvases. Through precise projection alignment technology, light and structure merge seamlessly to create breathtaking immersive visual experiences.

Yupitek has extensive experience executing large-scale projection mapping shows — from city hall facade events to corporate anniversary celebrations. We provide end-to-end services covering equipment planning, projector selection, and on-site technical support.

## Core Technologies

{{< card-group >}}
  {{< card title="Multi-Projector Blending & Alignment" >}}
    3D Mapping software precisely aligns projections onto complex architectural surfaces. Multiple projectors are seamlessly blended to eliminate visible seams and brightness variations.
  {{< /card >}}
  {{< card title="High-Brightness Outdoor Projection" >}}
    For outdoor architectural environments, we specify engineering projectors at 15,000+ lumens to deliver sharp, clear imagery even under ambient light interference.
  {{< /card >}}
  {{< card title="Real-Time Interactive Integration" >}}
    Combines motion sensors or audience interaction devices, turning viewers into active participants in the projection show to enhance the live experience.
  {{< /card >}}
