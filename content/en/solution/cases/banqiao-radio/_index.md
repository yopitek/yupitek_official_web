## Project Overview

**Location**: Banqiao Broadcasting Station, New Taipei City
**Type**: 360° Full-Immersive Projection — Permanent Exhibition
**Theme**: Japanese Colonial-Era Broadcasting History

Built during the Japanese colonial period, the Banqiao Broadcasting Station is one of Taiwan's most significant radio heritage sites. Working within strict historic preservation guidelines, Yupitek deployed a 360° Immersive projection system that transports visitors into the atmosphere of 1930s–1940s Taiwanese broadcasting culture.

## Technical Specifications

- **Projection Coverage**: Full wrap — ceiling and all four walls
- **Resolution**: Multi-projector blended output at ultra-high equivalent resolution
- **Projector Type**: Ultra-short-throw laser projectors (lamp-free, maintenance-free)
- **Control System**: Centralized management with one-touch on/off for venue staff

## Exhibit Content

- Historical recreation of radio studio scenes
- Life-in-Taiwan imagery from the Japanese colonial era
- Archival broadcast audio synchronized with visual presentations
