## Project Overview

**Location**: Taipei City Hall Plaza
**Type**: Architectural Projection Mapping (Building Mapping)
**Scale**: Large-format outdoor Projection Mapping show

The Taipei City Hall facade Projection Mapping show is one of Yupitek's landmark Architectural Projection Mapping projects. Built around a "Rainbow" theme, it transformed the City Hall building into a dynamic visual canvas. Precision multi-projector blending delivered a striking urban light art spectacle.

## Technical Challenges

- **Complex Architectural Geometry**: The multi-layered facade required precise 3D Mapping alignment across irregular surfaces
- **High-Brightness Outdoor Requirement**: Content remained clearly visible under urban ambient light conditions
- **Seamless Multi-Projector Blending**: Multiple high-brightness engineering projectors with edge blending, zero visible seams

## Equipment Used

- High-brightness engineering projectors (15,000+ lumens)
- 3D Mapping and blending control system
- IP-rated weatherproof enclosures
