## Project Overview

**Location**: Ming Chi University of Technology
**Type**: Interactive Touch Projection Wall
**Applications**: Academic showcase, classroom interaction, campus navigation

Ming Chi University of Technology deployed an interactive touch projection system, transforming the display space into an innovative collaborative learning environment for students and faculty. Users interact directly with the projected surface by hand, browsing course work, research data, and campus information.

## Technical Specifications

- **Touch Detection**: Infrared multi-point touch frame supporting simultaneous multi-user operation
- **Projection Size**: Large-format touch projection wall, far exceeding standard touchscreen size limitations
- **Content Management**: Intuitive back-end interface allowing instructors to update displayed content independently
- **System Integration**: Connected to the campus information system for real-time course information display
