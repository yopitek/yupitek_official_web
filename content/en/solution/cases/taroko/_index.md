## Project Overview

**Location**: Taroko Roller186 Roller Skating Rink
**Type**: Interactive Floor Projection System
**Theme**: Real-time interactive experience on the skating floor

Taroko Roller186 integrated interactive technology into its floor projection system. As skaters glide through the projection zone, the floor responds in real time with ripples, starbursts, particle effects, and other dynamic visuals — significantly boosting visitor engagement and venue appeal.

## Technical Highlights

- **Floor Interaction Detection**: Camera-based tracking of skater movement triggers real-time visual effects
- **Environment-Ready Equipment**: Dust- and moisture-resistant hardware designed for the humid skating rink environment
- **Low-Latency Response**: < 30 ms reaction time for smooth, natural interactive feedback
- **Multi-Theme Switching**: Seasonal themes can be swapped on demand (e.g., Christmas, Lunar New Year)
