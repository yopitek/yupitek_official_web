## Immersive Projection Spaces

Immersive space projection breaks the boundaries of traditional displays, placing audiences within a field of surrounding imagery. Whether for special museum exhibitions, corporate experience centers, or permanent tourist attraction installations, Yupitek delivers custom Immersive projection solutions.

## Technical Capabilities

{{< card-group >}}
  {{< card title="360° Panoramic Projection" >}}
    Full seamless projection across ceiling, floor, and all four walls — placing audiences inside the imagery for a completely Immersive visual experience.
  {{< /card >}}
  {{< card title="Dome Projection" >}}
    Hemispherical dome projection systems for planetariums, science education centers, and Immersive theaters, delivering stunning full-sky imagery.
  {{< /card >}}
  {{< card title="Non-Planar Projection Correction" >}}
