## Multi-Projector Blending Systems

When a single projector cannot meet the demands of an oversized or complex projection shape, multi-projector blending seamlessly stitches multiple projectors' images into a continuous, unified ultra-wide display.

Yupitek has extensive experience integrating multi-projector systems, with completed projects ranging from 2-projector setups to large-scale deployments of 20 or more units.

## Core Technologies

{{< card-group >}}
  {{< card title="Edge Blending" >}}
    Brightness gradient blending in the overlap zone between adjacent projectors eliminates visible seams for a seamless joined image.
  {{< /card >}}
  {{< card title="Geometric Correction (Warping)" >}}
    Geometric distortion correction for curved or non-planar projection surfaces, ensuring distortion-free imagery on any surface shape.
  {{< /card >}}
  {{< card title="Color Consistency Management" >}}
    Multi-projector color calibration and brightness uniformity ensures consistent color across the entire canvas, preventing warm-cool color variation.
  {{< /card >}}
{{< /card-group >}}
