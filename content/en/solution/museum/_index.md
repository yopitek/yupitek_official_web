## Museum Multimedia Exhibitions

Museums and cultural venues are a core service area for Yupitek's IT Application division. We deeply understand the unique requirements of exhibition spaces: long-term stable operation, low maintenance frequency, precise content management, and visual presentation aligned with the exhibition theme.

  {{< card title="Permanent Exhibition Multimedia Planning" >}}

  {{< /card >}}
  {{< card title="Historical Scene Digital Recreation" >}}

## Technical Advantages

- **Long-Life Light Source**: Laser projectors — no lamp replacement, extremely low maintenance cost
- **Silent Operation**: Low-noise design that does not disturb the quiet atmosphere of exhibitions
- **Remote Management**: Centralized network control for remote power scheduling and content updates
- **Color Calibration**: Meets museum-grade color accuracy requirements
